# TrueStone v1.9.2-pc

## Presets = Darkstone 1.05b difficulties
**Novice · Expert · Master · Hero · Legend** (replaces General / Advanced / Chaotic)

| Preset | Early monsters | Item power | Item LEVEL unlock |
|--------|----------------|------------|-------------------|
| **Novice** | ~62% scale, soft curve, dmg cap 16 | lag 1.12 (gear ahead) | −22 levels |
| **Expert** | ~80%, cap 22 | 0.95 | −12 |
| **Master** | ~95%, cap 28 | 0.85 | −8 |
| **Hero** | ~105%, cap 36 | 0.78 | −4 |
| **Legend** | ~120%, caps loose | 0.70 | off |

## Early-game feel (Novice default)
- Monster progression formula softens levels 1–15
- Early damage/AC caps stricter (threshold L≤16 on Novice)
- Items keep pace or beat monsters (`itemPowerLag ≥ 1`)
- High-tier item **LEVEL** requirements reduced so Expert/Master-tier drops/shops appear on Novice/Expert runs

## Note
Stock game difficulty select is still separate; these presets tune the **randomizer balance profile** to match 1.05b naming and intended challenge bands.
